c0001 Method and requirements

Aha Moment captured speech well, but the repo did not say why it is built this way, what must never break, or how a sentence becomes the starting context of an agent session. This change writes that layer down and adds a scan that keeps personal data out.

What changed

  • docs/METHOD.md: flywheel diagram, step table (10 steps), three memory layers, 10 invariants with enforcers, 10 failure modes, and a section on why this is context engineering.
  • docs/REQUIREMENTS.md: 16 requirements distilled from PLAN.md and README.md, including the data boundary.
  • tools/check_rules.py: leak scan (public patterns, private patterns from a gitignored file, home paths in the method layer, audio and transcript files in the tree).
